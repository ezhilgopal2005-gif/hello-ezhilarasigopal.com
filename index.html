Sudharva Marathon 2027

A 13-week social media plan to fill the start line for the hospital’s New Year marathon on 10 January 2027: what to post, how often and in which month.

View strategy deck ↗

Phase 1 · Oct 13–31

Launch

Announce the marathon and lock in early sign-ups. Driver: early-bird pricing.

  • Cinematic teaser reel
  • Doctor or founder on why the hospital hosts the run
  • Save-the-date, race categories and “How to register” carousel
2 reels + 3 posters / week
Phase 2 · November

Momentum

Turn interest into commitment and position the doctors as experts. Drivers: referrals and unlock reveals.

  • “Couch to 5K in 8 weeks” series
  • Training-plan and nutrition carousels
  • T-shirt and medal reveal, registration milestones
3 reels + 3 posters / week
Phase 3 · Dec 1–Jan 3

Last call

Create urgency and convert the fence-sitters. Drivers: group challenge and New Year push.

  • Route reveal and countdown reels
  • “Slots filling fast” alerts and FAQ carousel
  • New Year’s Day “your first win of 2027” push
3–4 reels + 4 posters / week, daily stories
Phase 4 · Jan 4–17

Race week

Race logistics, live coverage, then the thank-you wave.

  • Kit collection, checklist, parking and medical info
  • Live stories and same-day highlight reel
  • Thank-you post, albums, testimonials, “see you next year”

Brand Strategy · Creotodo for Muster Perfume

Muster Perfume

A brand brief and three-tier social media strategy written on behalf of Creotodo for Muster, an affordable-luxury fragrance brand built around identity and presence rather than just scent.

Open strategy PDF ↗
3

growth packages, from first presence to aggressive scale

5–20

Instagram reels a month, depending on the package

2–11

5–30

The brief

Identity over scentEmotion & memoryAffordable luxuryYoung, urban, visual audienceMinimal, controlled toneReal, non-AI contentUGC & organic influencers

Three packages

Package 1

Origin Essence

Start building brand presence and basic trust with consistent content.

  • 2 transformation, 1 trust and 2 lifestyle reels
  • 2 micro influencers on product exchange
  • Website claims: “Lasts 8–10 hours”, “High oil concentration”
  • Products grouped into Office, Date and Daily wear
5 reels + 6 posts / month, stories 3× a week
Package 2

Second Bloom

Build a consistent presence, lift reach and engagement, move towards steady growth.

  • Relatable reels on college, dating and daily life
  • “This smells like” posts and 3 featured customers a month
  • 4 micro influencers, mix of exchange and small paid
  • Reels added to the website homepage
8 reels + 10 posts / month, stories 4× a week
Package 3

Extrait de Essence

Scale aggressively: strong identity and recall, high engagement, better conversions.

  • 8 transformation reels as the core identity content
  • 8 micro + 3 mid-level influencers, campaign-based
  • Planned monthly shoots and batch production
  • 2 campaigns a month, e.g. Scent Switch, This Smells Like
20 reels + 10 posts / month, daily stories

Admission Strategy · Creotodo for FirstCry Intellitots

FirstCry Intellitots Velachery

A year-round admission strategy for a Montessori preschool: build trust with parents before admissions open, convert with urgency during the season, then keep families and turn them into social proof.

Open strategy PDF ↗
3

phases, from December to August

27

content and campaign ideas across the first two phases

10

kids per “Tiny Explorer” demo slot, run as an ad

₹1,000

off for every referred student

Phase 1 · Dec – Feb

Pre-admission

Awareness and trust building with parents.

  • “A Day at Intellitots” kid-height POV reel
  • Teacher intro reels and parent FAQ founder talks
  • 30-day toddler challenge on WhatsApp to grow reach
  • Walk-in tour with a 48-hour early-bird fee waiver
  • Tiny Explorer demo sessions, 10 kids a slot, ₹500 off
15 content ideas · Job: awareness + trust
Phase 2 · Mar – May

Admission

Conversions and urgency while admissions are open.

  • High-energy “Admissions Now Open” reel
  • Referral programme and a refundable one-week trial
  • Myth-busting and “curriculum in 15 seconds” reels
  • “How we handle separation anxiety” for anxious parents
  • “Seats filling fast” and “closing soon” urgency reels
12 content ideas · Job: conversions + urgency
Phase 3 · Jun – Aug

Post-admission

Retention and social proof once the new batch has joined.

  • Keep enrolled families engaged
  • Turn parent and child moments into social proof for the next cycle
Job: retention + social proof
